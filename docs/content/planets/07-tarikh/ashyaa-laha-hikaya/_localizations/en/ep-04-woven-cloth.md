## The Question It Answers

> **"Who taught people to make cloth?"**

## Learning Objective

**One goal:** the child explains that **a skill is passed from person to person**, and that **the thing alone does not teach how it was made.**

Criterion: tries weaving themselves, then states that they needed **a person to show them** rather than the thing alone, and orders the three weaving steps, in 3 tries of 4.

## 🔴 The Signature Decision: the episode does not complete the explanation

In this episode **the weaving is shown incomplete on purpose**: two steps are seen, and the third is not seen complete.

| ❌ rejected form | ✅ adopted form |
|---|---|
| a complete step-by-step explanation | 🔴 **two steps are shown, and the third needs someone to show it to you** |
| "and that is how you weave!" | 🔴 "**try it, and you will need someone to show you**" |
| an instructional video | 🔴 **an episode that ends with a need for a person** |

> 🔴 **This is the boldest decision in the series, and the hardest for the producer: an episode that does not complete what it began explaining.**
>
> **And the reason is that the goal itself is that.** Had the episode explained weaving in full, the child would learn **that the screen is enough** — the opposite of what it says: that a skill is passed from a person.
>
> **And the incompleteness is intentional and declared in the text**, not a writing error: "this step needs someone to show you, not someone to tell you."
>
> ⚠️ **This is why `interaction_mode` here is `guided` in truth, not in name.** The episode **cannot be fully understood without a person**, and this is recorded in the production notes.

## Parent Guide

> **What is your child learning?**
> That **a skill is passed from person to person**, and that the thing alone does not teach how it was made.
>
> **🔴 And the episode does not complete the explanation of weaving — on purpose**
> It shows two steps, then **stops** and says: "the third step needs someone to show you, not someone to tell you."
>
> This is the boldest thing in the series, and its reason is that **the goal itself is that**: had the episode explained weaving in full, your child would learn that **the screen is enough** — the opposite of what it says.
>
> **🔴 So the episode needs you, and cannot be fully understood without a present hand.** And you are the third step.
>
> **And you do not need to know weaving.** Show them the first step only: a thread passes over a thread. And they will continue, and err, and repeat.
>
> **🔴 And "how many times did they err before they learned?"**
> A line in the episode about the one who wove the piece, and it is intentional: it makes the maker **a person who learns**, not a skilled hand with no history. And your child will err in the activity, **and this is exactly what the episode says about others.**
>
> **⚠️ Safety note — please read it, and it is the most important in this series**
> 🔴 **The thread is the most dangerous thing in this episode.** The conditions:
>
> - 🔴 **No thread longer than 30 cm** — you cut them beforehand.
> - 🔴 **No thread is wrapped around a neck, a hand, a finger, a chair, or anything** — not even in play.
> - 🔴 **No full thread spool within their reach** ever.
> - 🔴 **No scissors, needle, or pin** in their hand.
> - 🔴 **No real loom** with moving parts or pegs.
> - 🔴 **An adult present the whole activity**, and the threads **are gathered and counted** after it and put away.
>
> **A question to talk about after the episode**
> "Who taught you to tie your shoes?" — 🔴 **and this is the closest example of the whole episode**, and it is from their life, not the past.

## Family Activity

> **A card and a weave — 15 minutes · 🔴 with an adult present · and short threads**
>
> **Materials:** a thick cardboard card the size of the palm or larger · 🔴 **6 threads each ≤ 30 cm, cut by an adult beforehand** · a pen.
>
> 1. 🔴 **The adult prepares the card**: short even slits on two opposite edges. 🔴 **The child cuts nothing.**
> 2. The adult stretches three threads between the slits **slightly taut** — these are the "threads that do not move."
> 3. 🔴 **Show them the first step with your hand**: a thread passes **over** the first.
> 4. Be silent, and let them complete: **under the second, then over the third.**
> 5. They will err in alternating once or twice. 🔴 **Do not correct with your hands** — repeat the first step only.
> 6. After they complete a row, ask: **"Could you have done this by looking at your shirt alone?"**
> 7. Ask: **"And who taught you just now?"**
> 8. 🔴 **Gather all the threads and count them**, and put them away. And do not leave a single thread.
>
> 🔴 **Steps 6 and 7 are the activity.** The weaving is a preamble to them, **and its beauty and completion do not matter.**
>
> **Variation for the older child (8 years):** 🔴 **they teach a younger sibling or someone else the first step** — and teaching someone is the lesson itself.
>
> ⚠️ **Conditions:** 🔴 each thread ≤ 30 cm · no wrapping around a neck, a hand, a finger, or anything · no full spool · no scissors, needle, or pin in their hand · no real loom · an adult present · and the threads are gathered, counted, and put away after the activity.

## Production Notes

- 🔴 **The motion stops at the third step and is not completed** · this is an **acceptance condition** and the most important decision in the series.
- 🔴 **The episode cannot be fully understood without a present person** · and `interaction_mode` here is `guided` **in truth, not in name.**
- 🔴 **It is not said "ask your mother" nor "watch a video"** · it is said "someone to show you," and the detail is in the parent guide.
- 🔴 **The prediction question has two correct answers** · and the following scene says so explicitly.
- 🔴 **The thread is the most dangerous element in the series**: each thread ≤ 30 cm · no wrapping around anything · no spool · and the threads are counted after the activity.
- 🔴 **The pack acknowledges its limit with an explicit message** · it is the only pack in the planet that does so.
- 🔴 **The second question is not posed before the activity** · the answer is from experience, not memorization.
- ⚠️ **Material-culture review required on** lines 6, 8, 12, 16, and 17.
- ⚠️ **Child-safety review required on** the thread-length limit · it is a safety point, not an editorial choice.
- ⚠️ **Language review:** "weaving" · "loom" · "crossings" · "stretched".
