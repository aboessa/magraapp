## The Question It Answers

> **"What do I do first? And what after it?"**

## Learning Objective

**One goal:** the child carries out **a two-action request** in the order they heard it: first they put the block in the basket, then they fold the towel once.

Criterion: carries out the two actions in the correct order in 3 tries of 4, after hearing the request twice and without splitting the request into two separate requests.

## 🔴 The Episode's Exception: two actions in a sentence

The series rule is **one action per sentence**. And this episode **breaks it on purpose**, because its very topic is the two-action sentence.

| Item | The rule in this episode |
|---|---|
| number of actions | 🔴 **two only** — never three |
| the connector | 🔴 **"then" alone** — not "and after" nor "and also" |
| the order | 🔴 **the first action is always easier** — so the child begins with a success |
| the distance | the two actions are **in two different places** — the movement fixes the order |

> 🔴 **The two actions in two different places is an educational decision.**
>
> A three-year-old remembers the order **with their body** before they remember it with their mind. So if the two actions are in the same place, they blur together. And if they walk two steps between them, the order becomes **a movement they live** rather than a list they memorize.
>
> ⚠️ **And the first action is always easier**, because a child who stumbles on the first forgets the second entirely.

## Parent Guide

> **What is your child learning?**
> To carry out **a two-action request in order**. This skill precedes almost everything: tidying toys, putting on shoes, and washing hands before eating.
>
> **The red line**
> 🔴 **No small pieces, no string, no ribbon, no plastic bag.** And the block in this episode has an **edge ≥ 6 cm** on purpose.
>
> 🔴 **And no running between the two places.** The distance is two steps in a clear space, and the floor has no obstacles.
>
> **How do you help them?**
> 🔴 **Ask for the two actions in one sentence, then be silent.** The most common error here is splitting the request: "Put the block" ... then after they finish: "Now fold the towel." This **cancels the exercise** — it became two separate requests, and they held no order.
>
> And if they do the second first, **do not correct immediately**. Repeat the request in the same words, and be silent. And Nabil himself starts with the second in the episode on purpose.
>
> **And start with the easier.** The first action must be the easier, because stumbling on it makes them forget the second entirely.
>
> **Name what they did:** "You put, then you folded."

## Family Activity

> **First then — 8 minutes · with an adult present**
>
> **Materials:** a wooden block or a sturdy plastic cup ≥ 8 cm · a basket · a cloth towel ≥ 30 cm.
>
> 1. Put the basket on one side, and the towel on another side, **two steps apart in a clear space**.
> 2. Ask in **one sentence**: "Put the block, then fold the towel."
> 3. 🔴 **Be silent for four seconds.** Do not repeat, and do not point.
> 4. If they need it, repeat the request **in exactly the same words**.
> 5. Name what they did: "You put, then you folded."
> 6. Swap the two actions in the second round: "Fold the towel, then put the block."
>
> 🔴 **Step 6 is the real test.** A child who memorized the movement will repeat it as it was, and one who understood the order **will listen to the new request**.
>
> **Variation for the older child (5 years):** three actions — 🔴 **and do not try it before the two actions are solid**.
>
> ⚠️ **The conditions:** a clear space with no obstacles on the floor · no running · no small pieces · no string or ribbon · away from stairs and outlets.

## Production Notes

- 🔴 **The two actions in two distant places on the screen** · and the movement between them is visible · for the place is what fixes the order.
- 🔴 **Nabil's error is an order error, not a movement error** — he starts with the second · and it is fixed by repeating the request, not by explaining.
- 🔴 **"then" is the only connector** in the episode · and its stability is a condition of acceptance and translation.
- 🔴 **The request is not split** in the text, the game, or the activity.
- ⚠️ **Translation:** the connector in each language is **one short word** indicating sequence · and two interchangeable words are not used.
- ⚠️ **Measurement limit:** ordering the two cards on the screen measures **perceiving the sequence**, and carrying out the two actions with the body is measured by the activity and by the parent's observation.
