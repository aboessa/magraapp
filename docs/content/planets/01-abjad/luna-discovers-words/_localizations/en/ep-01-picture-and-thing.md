## Parent Guide

> **What is your child learning?**
> That a picture stands for a real thing. This is the first step toward understanding that **a symbol carries meaning** — the foundation reading is built on later.
>
> **How to help?**
> Point to a thing at home and ask: "Where is its picture in the book?" — or the other way around. Repeat it with words they already know.
>
> **When to worry?**
> Don't worry if they don't answer at once. A three-year-old needs more time than a five-year-old. **Silence is thinking, not failure.**
>
> **What not to do?**
> Don't correct with "wrong". Say: "Let's look again together."

---

## Family Activity

> **Picture Hunt — 10 minutes**
>
> 1. Gather 4 things from home: an apple, a ball, a toy cat, a picture of a house.
> 2. Draw or print a picture of each thing.
> 3. Shuffle the pictures and ask your child to put each picture next to its thing.
> 4. **Swap roles:** they shuffle and you match — **and get one wrong on purpose** so they correct you. This builds their confidence.
>
> **No timer. No score. No winner.**
