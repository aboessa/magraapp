## Learning Objective

**One objective:** the child links the **spoken** word to the picture of the thing — with no picture in between.

Difference from Episode 1: there it was **picture → thing**. Here it is **sound → thing**. This moves from visual representation to sound representation — harder, and closer to reading.

Criterion: chooses 3 of 4 words correctly on first listening.

### Special visual rule for this episode

The episode is **audio-based**, so it must also be playable **visually**: as each word is spoken, a short `#00D6F5` **sound wave** appears to show that a sound is happening now. This is the required alternative to sound for a child who cannot hear.

## Parent Guide

> **What is your child learning?**
> That sound carries meaning. They heard "sun" and found the sun with no picture in between. This is the second step toward reading: **from sound to meaning**.
>
> **How to help?**
> In the car or the kitchen, say a word and ask them to point to the thing. No pictures — sound only.
>
> **When to worry?**
> If your child does not respond to spoken words **often** in daily life — not just in the app — see a hearing or speech specialist. The app **does not diagnose** and is not a substitute.
>
> **What not to do?**
> Don't repeat the word louder if they don't answer. Repeat it **slower**, not louder.

## Family Activity

> **A Word and a Thing — 10 minutes**
>
> 1. Sit together in one room.
> 2. Say a word for something in the room: "door".
> 3. Your child runs and touches the door.
> 4. Swap: they say and you touch.
> 5. **Add fun:** touch the wrong thing on purpose once and let them correct you.
>
> **No timer. Fun matters more than speed.**
