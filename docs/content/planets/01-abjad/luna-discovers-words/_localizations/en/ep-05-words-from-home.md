## Learning Objective

**One objective:** the child names familiar things from home and sorts them by room.

New here: not just matching but **classifying** — which object belongs where. This builds a network of meaning, not separate words.

Criterion: names 4 of 6 objects and sorts 3 of 4 correctly.

## Parent Guide

> **What is your child learning?**
> Not just the names of things, but **that things belong to places**. This builds a network of meaning in their mind instead of separate words, and makes vocabulary stick longer.
>
> **How to help?**
> This is the easiest episode to practise: **your home is the lesson**. While tidying a room, name things and ask: "Where does the spoon belong?"
>
> **When to worry?**
> A four-year-old should name most things in their home. If their vocabulary is very small and limited, talk with them **more** through the day — talking to a child builds vocabulary more than any app.
>
> **What not to do?**
> Don't quiz them. Turn it into ordinary talk during meals and tidying, not a question-and-answer session.

---

## Family Activity

> **A Walk Through the Home — 15 minutes**
>
> 1. Walk through the home room by room.
> 2. In each room, your child picks **one thing** and names it.
> 3. Ask: "Why is this thing here?" — let them think about the function, not the shape.
> 4. **Reverse game:** put a spoon on the bed on purpose and let them find the mistake and fix it.
>
> **Next step:** ask them to teach *you* the names of things in their room. **The one who teaches learns more.**
