## Assessment & Comprehension

Unit type `audio_card` — **no scoring and no comprehension questions, by design.** Remembrance is practiced, not tested. (The illustrated layer `illustrated_unit` is visual support only, with no recorded question.)

## Parent Guide

> Sit with your child and listen together. Do not ask them to repeat right now; quiet listening is the goal. Afterwards remind them of one meaning: "We praise God and ask Him to guide us." Silence is thinking, not failure.

## Family Activity (one)

> **Quiet Listening Corner — 5 minutes:** turn off distractions, listen to the licensed recitation together once, calmly, then gently ask your child: "How did you feel?" No timer and no grading.
