## Characters and Performance

- **Narrator:** quietly playful, marking movement with words rather than fast speech.
- **Droplet:** a small, confident voice, with no fake baby tone.

🔴 **Name adaptation:** "نُقطة" (the raindrop's name, literally "Droplet") is rendered **Droplet** — a meaningful English name matching the source, not a transliteration.

## Full Recording Script

**[A distant cloud and a light drizzle]**

Droplet was a small raindrop who lived in a big cloud. On a grey morning the cloud said: "It is time for rain."

The drops leaped, one after another.

**[Light, following taps of water]**

But Droplet said: "One moment! I want to know where I will land."

She looked down. She heard tree leaves moving. And she heard an umbrella opening. And she heard a little stream running.

She said: "So many places! Which one is waiting for me?"

A gentle breeze passed and said: "You will not know while you are in the cloud."

Droplet took a small breath, then let herself go to the air.

She fell… and fell… but she did not rush. The breeze carried her a little to the right, then a little to the left.

She passed near a red umbrella.

**[A splash of water on fabric, far away]**

She said: "Not this one."

And she passed near a window.

**[A single drop on glass]**

She said: "And not this one."

Then she landed on a big green leaf.

**[A soft drop on a leaf]**

Droplet rolled slowly across the leaf. At its edge she found another drop waiting.

The drop said: "Hello. We will jump together."

Droplet said: "To where?"

It said: "To the soil near the roots."

The two drops joined, and became a bigger drop. They grew a little heavier, then fell from the leaf.

**[A soft bloop into wet soil]**

The soil drank the water. And some of it reached the roots of the little plant.

Droplet said: "So this is the place that was waiting for me."

The breeze said from far away: "You found it because you began the journey."

Droplet was no longer in the cloud, and no longer alone. She was now with many drops in the soil, helping the little root.

And above her, the rain paused a while. The sun came out, and the leaf that had carried her at the start of the road gleamed.

As for Droplet, she was happy. She was a little late, yes, but once she moved she arrived exactly on time.

**[Three short melodic drops, then an ending]**

## Mixing Direction

- No thunder and no lightning. The rain is light and safe throughout the story.
- The place transitions are understood from the narrator; the child does not rely on the difference in effects alone.
- The phrase "the soil drank the water" is a simple description of water absorption, not a detailed science lesson.

## Parent Guide

> **A ~200-second audio story for a 3–5 child**
>
> An audio-only story — no pictures. A little raindrop wonders where she will land, lets herself begin the journey, and finds her place at the roots of a plant.
>
> **What it gives your child:** a gentle idea — that once you begin, the way reveals itself — with **no danger** (the rain is light, no thunder, no lightning) and no lesson stated at the end. The action carries the meaning.
>
> **How to use it?** A calm daytime or rest-time listen. If your child asks "does the soil really drink?" a simple answer is enough: "Yes, plants drink water through their roots" — the science comes later.
