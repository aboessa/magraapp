## Characters and Performance

- **Narrator:** warm, close, and curious without exaggeration.
- **Salma:** a calm child, her words short and clear.
- **The grandmother:** a reassuring, slightly slow voice.

🔴 **Name adaptation:** "سلمى" → **Salma**, "عمر" → **Omar**, substituted locally where needed.

## Full Recording Script

**[Series sting 1.5s, then the light sound of paper being folded]**

Salma said: "Grandma, what is this little box?"

The grandmother said: "This is the box of little sounds."

Salma shook the box.

**[A light ring: ting… ting]**

She said: "I heard something! Is there a bell in the box?"

The grandmother laughed and said: "We will not open it now. We will listen first."

Salma sat close to her grandmother. She put the box between them. The house was quiet, so quiet that Salma heard the clock.

**[A quiet tick, twice]**

She said: "Tick… tick… that is the clock."

Then a sound came from near the window.

**[A short, soft chirp]**

Salma said: "And that is a bird."

And from the kitchen came another sound.

**[Two spaced-out drops of water]**

She said: "Drip… drip… the water is dripping."

The grandmother said: "Wonderful. You knew three sounds without seeing them."

Salma was quiet for a moment. Then she brought the box close to her ear, and shook it gently.

**[Ting… ting… then a small wooden rub]**

She said: "There is something that rings, and something else that moves."

The grandmother said: "What do you think it is?"

Salma said: "Maybe a small bell… and a piece of wood."

The grandmother opened the box slowly.

**[The light click of a wooden lid]**

Salma reached in, and took out a small blue bell, and a wooden block.

She moved the bell.

**[Ting… ting]**

Then she moved the block inside the box.

**[Knock… knock]**

She said happily: "I knew them with my ears!"

The grandmother said: "Your ears listen, and your mind gathers the clues."

Salma put into the box a small spoon, a big button, and a piece of cloth. Then she closed the lid.

Her brother Omar came in, and she told him: "This is the box of little sounds. We will not open it now. We will listen first."

Omar shook it gently.

**[A faint metal ring and the rub of cloth]**

He said: "I hear a spoon… and something soft."

Salma smiled. Now they had a new puzzle, one that needs no picture, only two listening ears.

**[One gentle bell, then a short musical ending]**

## Mixing Direction

- The effects are a helpful clue, but the narrator names the source of each sound afterward, so the story does not depend on the child telling them apart alone.
- No silence longer than 1.2s. The bell has no sharpness and no sudden rise.
- No interactive questions that pause playback; the grandmother's question is part of the dialogue and its answer is present.

## Parent Guide

> **A ~180-second audio story for a 3–5 child**
>
> An audio-only story — no pictures on purpose. Salma and her grandmother play a listening game: guessing sounds without seeing them.
>
> **What it gives your child:** attentive listening, and the small joy of recognizing the world by ear. There is no danger and no lesson stated at the end.
>
> **How to use it?** A calm daytime listen. Afterward you can play the same game at home: put small safe objects in a box and guess them by their sound.
