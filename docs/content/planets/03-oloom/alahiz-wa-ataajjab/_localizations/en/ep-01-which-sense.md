## The Question It Answers

> **"How did I know that?"**

## Learning Objective

**One objective:** the child names the sense that told them about the thing — the eye, the ear, or the hand.

Criterion: links 3 of 3 situations to the correct sense, in 3 of 4 attempts, after one prompt with no help.

```
    [4 seconds of silence]

    [4 seconds of silence]

24. And your ear tells you about sounds.

    [4 seconds of silence]

```

**30 sentences · each sentence ≤ 7 words · ~90 words/minute.**

### Delivery Notes

| # | Note |
|---|---|
| 1 | 🔴 Calm, warm, **not dramatic** · ~90 words/minute |
| 2 | Tone of an **invitation**, not a command |
| 3 | 🔴 Tone of **reassurance** — a safety item phrased as a description |
| 4 | 🔴 **One instruction per sentence** · then **complete silence** |
| 5 | 🔴 "**Gently**" with emphasis |
| 6 | 🔴 Tone of **recording** — no surprise and no victory |
| 7 | 🔴 A genuine question · then 4 seconds of silence |
| 8 | 🔴 Praise for **noticing**, not for being right |

## Parent Guide

> **What is your child learning?**
> That **the eye, the ear, and the hand tell them different things**. And this is the first step in scientific observation: for the child to know **where their information came from**.
>
> **🔴 The red line**
> - 🔴 **No tasting and no smelling.** The episode does not ask for them, **and neither does the whole series**. If your child brings something near their mouth, move it away calmly and say: "We only look and touch."
> - 🔴 **Everything on the table is bigger than their fist.** No button, no coin, no bead.
> - 🔴 **No glass and no ceramic.** The cup is plastic.
> - 🔴 **Nothing hot, and no stove or kettle in the room.**
> - 🔴 **No cord or socket within their reach.**
> - 🔴 **No standing on a chair.** Seated on the floor or at a low table.
>
> **What exactly do you do?**
> **You choose the three things and place them** before playing, and you sit with them. The episode is designed so that **your hand does the action first** and they copy you — so do it with them in scene 3.
>
> **How do you help?**
> - **Let the silence pass.** Four seconds feels long to you, and is barely enough for them.
> - **Don't answer for them.** If they go quiet, repeat the prompt in the same words.
> - **Don't correct with the word "wrong".** Say: "Touch it again."
> - **Name the sense after the action, not before.** "You touched it… your hand told you."
>
> **Why three senses, not five?**
> Because taste and smell **lead a three-year-old to bring what they don't know near their mouth**. This is a deliberate decision across the whole series, and the five senses come at an older age.
>
> **When do you stop the episode?**
> If your child still puts things in their mouth, **watch the episode with no objects on the table** — by looking only. The episode works with no materials.

## Family Activity

> **Who Told You? — 5 minutes · no screen**
>
> 1. 🔴 **You choose** three big, safe things from home and place them on a low table.
> 2. Sit together. You touch first, and say: "My hand told me."
> 3. Ask them for one action: "Look" or "Listen" or "Touch gently".
> 4. 🔴 **Stay silent for four seconds after each prompt.** Don't repeat it during them.
> 5. After they do it, ask: "**Who told you?**" and accept their pointing to their eye, ear, or hand.
> 6. Return the things to their place together, then wash hands.
>
> 🔴 **Step 4 is the activity.** The silence is what produces the action.
>
> 🔴 **No tasting. No smelling. Nothing smaller than their fist. No glass. Nothing hot.**
>
> **A variation for the older child (5 years):** close their eyes, put one of the three things in their hand, and ask: "What is it? And who told you?" — 🔴 **on condition that the three things are known to them and they touched them a moment ago.**

- The observe-and-name-the-source method, and the three-sense scope, are universal and translate fully.
- English says "the eye / the ear / the hand" with the definite article, matching the source's naming of the organ rather than the abstract "sense".
- 🔴 The safety-boundary lines ("all safe", "touch gently", "no tasting") keep their emphasis exactly and are not softened.
- 🔴 `alahiz.ep1.safe` and `alahiz.ep1.gentle` are mandatory in every language — they are safety items, not dialogue.
- ⚠️ The three things are substitutable per market, on condition: **big · plastic or cloth · no edges · and not eaten**.
