## The Situation

Salma plays with her friends. A new girl stands by the wall alone. The game is about to start.

## The Value

**Kindness.**

## Learning Objective

**One objective:** the child learns that **one short word is enough** to change a situation, and that he needs neither a long sentence nor a talent for talk.

The criterion: he matches 6 situations to a suitable kind word, in 3 of 4 attempts.

## 🔴 Two Decisions That Define This Episode

### 1. The episode is **generative, not cautionary**

| ❌ The rejected form | ✅ The form used |
|---|---|
| "A hurtful word wounds" | "A kind word is enough" |
| Showing a child wounded by a word | Showing a child **invited** by a word |
| "Don't say..." | "Say..." |
| **The crumpled-paper metaphor** | 🔴 **Fully rejected** |

> 🔴 **Rejecting the metaphor "a crumpled paper never returns as it was"** — which is very common in children's content.
>
> **Why:** it teaches that harm is **final and unrepairable.** And a seven-year-old who said a hurtful word yesterday comes away with **helplessness and shame**, not a motive to repair.
>
> And repair is **possible** — that is the subject of [Episode 6](./ep-06-i-apologize.md). This episode builds the skill **before** it is needed: to find the word, not to fear it.

### 2. The word is **very short**

| Length | Example |
|---|---|
| ✅ Used | "Play with us?" — **two words** |
| ❌ Rejected | "Hello, I'm Salma, and I noticed you standing alone, so would you like to..." |

**Why:** the shy child does not begin because he thinks he needs a **speech.** And when he sees that **two words are enough**, the act becomes possible.

**And this is the real learning objective:** not "be kind" — but **"kindness is cheap, short, and within your reach."**

    [pause 2s]

    [pause]

    [pause]

26. And it needs no many words.
```

**26 sentences** — ⚠️ below the 30–36 range, **and the exception is intentional**: the episode teaches that **little is enough**, and lengthening the talk in it contradicts its message. The duration is filled with shots and pauses.

## Parent Guide

> **What is your child learning?**
> That **two words are enough.** Salma said: "Play with us?" — and needed no more.
>
> **🔴 Why doesn't the episode talk about hurtful words?**
> On purpose. The episode is **generative**: it teaches what is **said**, not what is avoided.
>
> **And we rejected the metaphor "a crumpled paper never returns as it was"** — which is very common. Its reason is that it teaches harm is **final and unrepairable**, so it produces in a child who said a hurtful word yesterday **shame and helplessness**, not a motive to repair.
>
> And repair is **possible**, which is the subject of [the next episode](./ep-06-i-apologize.md).
>
> **The real obstacle for your child is not a lack of kindness**
> but his thinking that he needs a **speech.** Salma thought: "**and I don't know what to say**" — and this is the commonest obstacle.
>
> So the narrator counts the words explicitly: "she said only **two words**" then "**Two words.** And that was all it took."
>
> **And the most dangerous obstacle**
> "**Maybe someone else will invite her.**" It seems reasonable — but when everyone waits for someone else to start, no one starts. Ask your child about it specifically.
>
> **🔴 Why is the girl's aloneness only "her first day"?**
> Because any other reason — a different look, a different language, a disability — would turn the episode into a **lesson about difference** and produce stereotyping. And the new girl **does not cry and does not seem miserable**: no pity, because pity makes kindness a favor from above, and what is required is **an invitation among equals.**
>
> **🔴 A safety clause**
> The episode teaches **inviting a child like himself to play in a supervised place** — and the wall in the episode is **inside the yard**, not in an isolated corner. Kindness **does not mean** talking to just anyone, nor going to a secluded place to help someone.
>
> **How do you help him?**
> Teach him **three short sentences he memorizes**: "Play with us?" · "Can I help?" · "My name is...". **A memorized sentence gets said in the moment, an un-memorized one stays in the head.**
>
> **The episode's moment**
> After Salma stays where she is, your child is asked: "**What would you do?**" and **eight seconds of silence.**
>
> **And if your child is shy?**
> Don't push him, and don't call him shy in front of people. Start with the smallest step: **a smile**, or a one-word greeting. And tell him: "One word is enough today." — shrinking the step is what makes it possible.

## Family Activity

> **Three Sentences in My Pocket — 15 minutes**
>
> 1. Write together **three short sentences** on a small note he carries in his bag:
>    - "Play with us?"
>    - "Can I help?"
>    - "My name is..."
> 2. 🔴 **Each sentence three words or fewer.** A long one is not said.
> 3. Practice them **by role-play**: you are the standing child, and he invites you. Then swap.
> 4. Try each sentence **three times** until it is easy on his tongue.
> 5. After a week ask: "Did you say one of them? What happened?"
>
> **The role-play practice is the activity.** The sentence spoken at home gets spoken at school, others do not.
>
> **The Noticing Game — 10 minutes**
>
> At the end of the day, everyone mentions **a kind word they heard**, not said. You too.
>
> **Noticing before acting.** A child who pays attention to the kind words around him finds them in himself faster.
>
> **A question to discuss:** "What is the nicest word anyone said to you?" — recalling it makes him want to give it.
>
> 🔴 **What we don't do:** we don't force him to invite anyone, we don't call him shy, and we don't reward him for a kind word. **And we don't ask him to help anyone in a place we can't see.**

say.nice_try        = Nice try
say.thank_you       = Thank you
```

⚠️ **The names** (Salma) are replaced locally.

🔴 **The six sentences are phrased in each language in the shortest natural form possible** — **three words or fewer.** A long form contradicts the episode's message, and is not translated literally but chosen as the commonest form among that market's children.

⚠️ **The invitation form is reviewed culturally:** the way to invite a child to play differs between communities, and what is actually said is chosen.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Duration 240 seconds ±10.
- [ ] Every sentence ≤ 12 words · **26 sentences** — ⚠️ below the range, **and the exception is justified and documented.**
- [ ] 🔴 **The value's name in sentence 25 only.**
- [ ] 🔴 **No preaching**: no "you must be kind."
- [ ] 🔴 **The pause question** with a unified frame and **total silence 8 seconds.**
- [ ] 🔴 **The episode is generative**: no hurtful word shown, no classification of harm.
- [ ] 🔴 **The crumpled-paper metaphor is absent** — nor any metaphor of unrepairable harm.
- [ ] 🔴 **The word is two words** — and the narrator counts them explicitly twice.
- [ ] 🔴 The sentence **"Two words. And that was all it took."** is present.
- [ ] **The three obstacles** are present, **in the same tone**, including **"maybe someone else will invite her."**
- [ ] 🔴 The reason for aloneness is **"her first day" only** — not a look nor language nor disability.
- [ ] 🔴 The new girl **without crying and without misery and without pity.**
- [ ] 🔴 **The wall inside the yard**, not in an isolated corner.
- [ ] "And Salma did not miss her turn" — **correcting an expectation, not a reward.**
- [ ] 🔴 **No reward**: no applause and no praise · and the effect is **the act spreading.**
- [ ] The other-day scene is **short, no dialogue.**
- [ ] The definition **"And it needs no many words"** — lowers the obstacle.
- [ ] The `long-speech` distractor = the targeted obstacle.
- [ ] `safety_notes` states **inviting a child like himself in a supervised place.**
- [ ] 🔴 **No religious text** · the setting is neutral.
- [ ] The family activity: **three sentences ≤ 3 words** · role-play practice · no forcing and no reward.
- [ ] The parent guide forbids calling the child shy, and warns against helping someone in an unseen place.
- [ ] 🔴 **Pedagogical review** documented.
- [ ] ⚠️ **Cultural-neutrality review** of the invitation forms and names.
- [ ] ⚠️ English language review: "two words" · "that was all it took" · "did not miss."
- [ ] No flashing > 3Hz · no sudden sound.
