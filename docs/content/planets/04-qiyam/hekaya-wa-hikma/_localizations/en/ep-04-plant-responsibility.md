## The Situation

Yusuf took a small plant to care for. He watered it two days, then forgot for three days. So its leaves wilted.

## The Value

**Responsibility.**

## Learning Objective

**One objective:** the child learns that responsibility is **a system you build**, not a memory you strain.

The criterion: he sets a care schedule in the simulation and keeps the plant alive 7 days, in 3 of 4 attempts.

## 🔴 Two Decisions That Define This Episode

### 1. The plant **does not die**

| ❌ The rejected form | What it produces |
|---|---|
| The plant dies because of his forgetting | 🔴 **Untreatable guilt** in a seven-year-old |
| "You killed it with your neglect" | Fear of taking on any responsibility |
| A scene of a dead, dried plant | Trauma with no way out |

| ✅ The form used | What it produces |
|---|---|
| **Wilts then revives** | Repair is possible |
| "Its leaves drooped" | Visible harm but treatable |
| Returns within two days | **The effect of the right action is visible** |

> 🔴 **A plant's death teaches the child that a mistake is final.** And a seven-year-old lacks the tools to process this guilt, so he ends up avoiding responsibility instead of bearing it.
>
> **Wilting then reviving** gives the same causality lesson and leaves a door open for repair — which is what we want him to learn about himself.

### 2. Responsibility is **a system**, not memory

| ❌ The rejected solution | ✅ The taught solution |
|---|---|
| "I'll try to remember" | **A note on the door** |
| "I'll be more attentive" | Linking watering to an existing habit *(after breakfast)* |
| Relying on intention | Relying on an **external reminder** |

> 🔴 **"I'll try to remember" is not a solution.** A child — and an adult — forgets. And real responsibility is **building something that reminds you**, not straining your memory.
>
> This lesson moves the value from a **moral trait** the child either has or doesn't, to a **learnable skill.** And the difference is decisive: the trait discourages those who lack it, the skill is learned.

## Parent Guide

> **What is your child learning?**
> That responsibility is **a system you build**, not a memory you strain. Yusuf did not say "I'll try to remember" — **he wrote a note and stuck it on his room's door.**
>
> **🔴 Why did the plant not die?**
> On purpose, and this is an important decision.
>
> A plant dying because of a child's forgetting produces **guilt he has no tools to process**, so he ends up **avoiding responsibility** instead of bearing it. In the episode it **wilts then revives** — the same causality lesson, and an open door for repair.
>
> **🔴 The trap in the episode**
> Yusuf thinks of three solutions, one of which is: "**I'll try to remember tomorrow.**" It looks right, and it is what most children say — and adults.
>
> **But it is not a solution.** We all forget. And real responsibility is to **make something that reminds you.**
>
> This moves the value from a **trait** your child has or lacks, to a **skill he learns.** The difference is decisive: the trait discourages those who lack it, the skill is learned.
>
> **What this means for you practically**
> If your child forgets a task, the question is not "why did you forget?" but "**what will remind you next time?**"
>
> And help him build the reminder: a note on the door · a picture on the mirror · linking the task to an existing habit *(right after breakfast)*. **Linking to an existing habit is the strongest reminder for children** — because it needs no remembering at all.
>
> **And notice the note's location in the episode**
> On the **room's door** — he passes it every morning without meaning to. **A good system works without effort.** A note in a drawer is not read.
>
> **🔴 Before you give him a plant**
> - **A non-toxic plant only.** Many ornamental plants are toxic if swallowed, so ask when buying.
> - **No eating leaves or soil.** And wash hands after every touch.
> - **No over-watering** — drowning kills more than drought. Fix a set amount *(a small cup)*.
> - Start with a **forgiving** plant that tolerates two days of forgetting.
>
> **The episode's moment**
> After Yusuf thinks of the three solutions, your child is asked: "**What would you do?**" and **eight seconds of silence.**
>
> **If he says "I'll try to remember"?**
> Don't correct him at once. Ask: "**And if you forget again?**" — let him reach on his own that intention is not enough.

---

## Family Activity

> **One Task and One System — a week**
>
> 1. Choose **only one task** for your child, small and clear: watering a plant · feeding a fish · packing his bag in the evening.
> 2. **One task, not three.** Multiple tasks all fail.
> 3. Ask him: "**What will remind you?**" and let him suggest.
> 4. Make the reminder together: a note **in his handwriting** · or a picture he draws · or linking it to an existing habit.
> 5. Hang it in **a place he passes without meaning to** — not in a drawer nor a notebook.
> 6. After a week ask: "Did the reminder work? And what do we change in it?"
>
> **The sixth question is the activity.** Improving the system is a higher skill than keeping to it.
>
> 🔴 **No prize for keeping to it.** The effect is **the result itself**: the plant is green, the bag is ready.
>
> **If he forgets?** No punishment, no scolding. The only question: "**What didn't work in the reminder?**" — and the answer fixes the system.
>
> **A question to discuss:** "Do grown-ups forget?" — yes, a lot. **Show him your own reminder**: your phone, or a list on the fridge. Seeing his father use a system fixes the lesson more than any sentence.

⚠️ **The names** (Yusuf) are replaced locally.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Duration 240 seconds ±10.
- [ ] Every sentence ≤ 12 words · **29 sentences** — the exception is acceptable *(scene 2 is visual)*.
- [ ] 🔴 **The value's name in sentence 27 only.**
- [ ] 🔴 **No preaching**: no "you must be responsible" nor "the negligent child."
- [ ] 🔴 **The pause question** with a unified frame and **total silence 8 seconds.**
- [ ] 🔴 **The plant wilts and does not die** — "drooped," not "died" nor "dried up."
- [ ] 🔴 **The revival is gradual over two days**, not instant.
- [ ] 🔴 Forgetting **in a tone of description, not accusation** · ordinary busyness shots.
- [ ] 🔴 Yusuf **without crying or a guilty face.**
- [ ] 🔴 **The third choice "I'll try to remember"** is present, **in the same tone** as the others, **and its weakness is not exposed in the voice.**
- [ ] 🔴 The solution is a **written external reminder**, not intention.
- [ ] 🔴 The sentence **"So he did not rely on his memory alone"** is present.
- [ ] The note is **in a child's handwriting** and on the **room's door.**
- [ ] 🔴 **No reward** — "Yusuf did not get a prize. But his plant came back."
- [ ] 🔴 The definition at the end is **two sentences with a contrast**: "It is not to always remember... but to make something that reminds you."
- [ ] 🔴 The `try-remember` distractor is **present** in the first question.
- [ ] `safety_notes` states a **non-toxic** plant and forbids eating leaves and soil and requires hand-washing and no over-watering.
- [ ] 🔴 **No religious text** · the setting is neutral.
- [ ] The family activity is **one task only** · **no prize** · and includes **improving the system.**
- [ ] The parent guide forbids the "why did you forget?" question and suggests "what will remind you?".
- [ ] The link with the Science planet is documented, **and the difference in goal is shown.**
- [ ] 🔴 **Pedagogical review** documented.
- [ ] ⚠️ **Plant-safety review** of the plant species per market.
- [ ] ⚠️ English language review: "water it" · "had drooped."
- [ ] No flashing > 3Hz · no sudden sound.
